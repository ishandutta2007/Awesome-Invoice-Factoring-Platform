# Awesome-Invoice-Factoring-Platform

## Top Invoice Factoring Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Invoice Financing, Receivables Purchase, Supply Chain Finance, Working Capital & Early Payment Solutions*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Invoice Factoring** and related receivables financing. These platforms allow businesses to sell or finance outstanding invoices for immediate cash, improve working capital, or participate in supply-chain finance and dynamic discounting programs.



**Examples** include FundThrough, Resolve, TreviPay, BlueVine, Drip Capital, Fundbox, PrimeRevenue, Taulia, C2FO, and Resolve Pay (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: True end-to-end open-source invoice factoring platforms are extremely rare. Factoring involves capital markets, credit risk, regulatory compliance, and funding sources that are difficult to replicate in pure open-source form. This section therefore highlights prototypes, blockchain-based experiments, invoicing foundations, and building blocks that teams can use to explore or partially automate receivables financing workflows.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[FundThrough](https://www.fundthrough.com/)**  

  Invoice factoring platform that lets businesses factor individual invoices with no long-term contracts, offering flexible, on-demand access to working capital.



- **[Resolve / Resolve Pay](https://resolvepay.com/)**  

  B2B trade credit and invoice financing solutions that help sellers offer net terms while getting paid quickly, with buyer credit underwriting.



- **[TreviPay](https://www.trevipay.com/)**  

  Global B2B payments and trade credit platform that supports invoice financing, accounts receivable management, and buyer/seller networks.



- **[BlueVine](https://www.bluevine.com/)**  

  Popular small-business financing platform offering invoice factoring (and lines of credit) with competitive advance rates and relatively fast approval.



- **[Drip Capital](https://www.dripcapital.com/)**  

  Trade and export invoice financing platform focused on cross-border receivables, particularly strong for exporters in emerging markets.



- **[Fundbox](https://www.fundbox.com/)**  

  Working-capital platform that provides invoice-based financing and credit lines designed for small businesses needing quick cash against receivables.



- **[PrimeRevenue](https://www.primerevenue.com/)**  

  Enterprise supply-chain finance and working-capital platform used by large corporates to run supplier financing and early-payment programs.



- **[Taulia (SAP)](https://taulia.com/)**  

  Working-capital and supply-chain finance platform (now part of SAP) that enables dynamic discounting and supplier early-payment programs.



- **[C2FO](https://www.c2fo.com/)**  

  Global working-capital marketplace that connects buyers and suppliers for early payment and invoice financing through competitive auctions and programs.



- **[Other notable platforms](https://www.fundthrough.com/)**  

  Additional players in the broader invoice financing and supply-chain finance space include various regional and vertical specialists serving trucking, staffing, manufacturing, and export markets.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



> **Note on scarcity**: There are currently no mature, production-ready open-source platforms that fully replicate commercial invoice factoring (complete with funding, credit underwriting, and regulatory compliance). The projects below are prototypes, research systems, blockchain experiments, or foundational invoicing tools.



- **[CashFlowNow](https://github.com/nlankelis/CashFlowNow)**  

  Web-based invoice factoring prototype that allows SMEs and freelancers to upload invoices (with OCR), receive cash offers, and track factoring deals.



- **[Invoice Liquidity Network (ILN)](https://github.com/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/Invoice-Liquidity-Network)**  

  Open-source, decentralized invoice factoring protocol built on Stellar/Soroban smart contracts. Enables on-chain invoice submission, funding by liquidity providers, and settlement.



- **[ZK-Factor](https://github.com/imajus/zk-factor)**  

  Privacy-preserving, blockchain-based invoice factoring platform that uses zero-knowledge proofs to prevent double-factoring while keeping sensitive details private.



- **[Oreko](https://github.com/orekoapp/oreko)**  

  Open-source, self-hosted quote and invoice builder (alternative to commercial invoicing tools) that can serve as a foundation for receivables tracking.



- **[Summit Finance & similar invoicing apps](https://summitfinance.app/)**  

  Modern open-source invoicing and financial management applications that handle invoice creation, tracking, and basic receivables workflows.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo Accounting](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Fully open-source ERPs with strong accounts-receivable, invoicing, and payment-tracking modules that can be extended for internal factoring or early-payment workflows.



- **[Invoice data extraction & OCR toolkits](https://github.com/search?q=invoice+ocr+OR+invoice+extraction)**  

  Open-source document AI and OCR projects commonly used as the capture layer in any custom receivables or factoring pipeline.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Smart contract & DeFi primitives**: Libraries and protocols for tokenizing or funding invoices on public or permissioned ledgers.

- **Credit scoring & risk models**: Open-source or research implementations of receivables risk assessment (usually requiring proprietary data to be useful).

- **Workflow & approval engines**: Tools for routing invoice financing requests through internal approval and funding steps.

- **Accounting integrations**: Connectors that push financed invoice status into open-source ledgers (ERPNext, Odoo, GnuCash, etc.).

- **Marketplace prototypes**: Community experiments in matching invoice sellers with funders.

- Various fintech research projects exploring **receivables tokenization** and **supply-chain finance** on blockchain.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Use an open-source ERP (**ERPNext** or **Odoo**) as the system of record for invoices and customers.  

Add document AI / OCR for invoice intake.  

For experimental or internal “factoring,” implement a request → approval → funding workflow with clear audit trails.  

Blockchain-based approaches (ILN, ZK-Factor style) can be explored for transparency and double-spend prevention, but they still require real capital and legal structures to function as actual factoring.  

In almost all production cases, the capital and regulatory layers remain outside pure open-source software.
